A Modern Platform for Your Restaurant

This proposal outlines a plan to build you a custom, commission-free ordering application. Below are three carefully crafted packages designed to fit your needs and budget, from getting started to scaling up.

Detailed MVP Packages

Starter MVP

The essential foundation to get your restaurant online, professionalize your takeaway service, and eliminate errors from phone orders.

Online Menu & Shopping Cart

Why it's important: Gives your customers a beautiful, easy-to-navigate digital menu. They can browse, add items to a cart, and order without pressure, often leading to larger order sizes.

Cash on Delivery Order System

Why it's important: The simplest way to start accepting online orders. It provides a structured, error-free order record for your kitchen, avoiding misunderstandings from phone calls.

Admin Dashboard for Order Management

Why it's important: This is your control center. Your cashier can see all incoming orders in one place, accept them, and update their status (e.g., from "Processing" to "Ready for Pickup").

PDF Ticket Printing

Why it's important: Creates a standardized, professional ticket for every order. This ensures your kitchen staff gets clear, legible instructions every time, reducing mistakes and speeding up preparation.

Basic Menu Management

Why it's important: You have control. If a price changes or you add a new dish, you can update the menu yourself instantly without needing to call a developer.

Recommended

Lean MVP

Builds on the Starter foundation with automation and enhanced customer experience features, creating a smoother, more professional operation.

Includes all Starter MVP features, plus:

Real-time Order Dashboard

Why it's important: This is a game-changer. Orders appear instantly with a notification sound, no manual refreshing needed. It ensures your staff reacts immediately, crucial during busy hours.

Customer Order Tracking Page

Why it's important: Reduces customer anxiety and "where is my order?" phone calls. Customers can see the status of their order on their own, improving their experience and freeing up your staff.

Automatic Email Confirmations

Why it's important: Provides customers with immediate reassurance that their order was received correctly. It's a mark of professionalism that builds trust and provides them with a digital receipt.

Basic Sales Analytics Dashboard

Why it's important: Turns your sales data into insights. You can instantly see how many orders you've had, your daily revenue, and which items are most popular, helping you make smarter business decisions.

Complete MVP

The most robust package, designed for restaurants looking to optimize operations, handle more volume, and prepare for future growth.

Includes all Lean MVP features, plus:

Full Multi-language Support (French & Arabic)

Why it's important: Caters to all your customers in their preferred language. This improves accessibility and shows a high level of customer care, making your restaurant more welcoming to a wider audience.

Admin Role Management

Why it's important: Enhances security and control. You can create different user roles, for example, a "Cashier" who can manage orders but not change menu prices, and an "Admin" (you) who has full access.

End-of-Day "Z-Report" Generation

Why it's important: Simplifies your accounting. At the end of a shift or day, you can generate a summary report with one click, showing total sales, number of orders, and other key metrics for easy reconciliation.

Our 8-Week Development Roadmap

We will work together through a structured, transparent process. Here’s what the journey to launch looks like:

WEEKS 1-2

Strategic Kick-off & Blueprinting

We'll finalize the plan, create visual wireframes of the app, and set up the technical foundation. This ensures we both agree on the vision before any code is written.

WEEKS 3-4

Building the Control Center

I will build the core of the admin dashboard, focusing on the menu management system. You'll be able to start adding your menu items during this phase.

WEEKS 5-6

Developing the Customer Experience

This is where the customer-facing app comes to life. I'll build the menu, cart, and checkout flow. We'll be able to place our first test order by the end of this phase.

WEEKS 7-8

Testing, Deployment & Handover

We'll rigorously test every feature, fix any bugs, and deploy the application to your live domain. I will then conduct a training session with you and your staff before the official handover.
